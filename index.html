Frequently Asked Questions

Porcelain veneers typically last 10–15 years with proper care, including regular brushing, flossing, and dental checkups. Avoiding habits like nail-biting or chewing hard objects can extend their lifespan.

The implant placement surgery takes about 1–2 hours. However, the full process including healing and crown placement can take 3–6 months. Dr. Jana will walk you through a personalized timeline at your consultation.

Modern root canal treatment is comfortable and performed under local anesthesia. Most patients feel little to no pain during the procedure, and it helps relieve the discomfort caused by infected teeth.

Cavities are treated by removing the decayed part of the tooth and restoring it with a tooth-colored filling. Early treatment helps prevent pain and more extensive procedures later on.

Most patients benefit from a professional cleaning every six months. Regular cleanings remove plaque and tartar buildup, helping prevent gum disease and keeping your smile healthy.

Dental crowns are recommended to restore teeth that are weakened, cracked, heavily filled, or treated with a root canal. They protect the tooth and restore its strength, function, and appearance.

Tooth extraction is recommended when a tooth is severely damaged, infected, or cannot be restored. Dr. Jana always explores conservative treatment options first and ensures the procedure is as comfortable as possible.
